Bristol, since 2019

A glasshouse, not a factory.

Nocturne & Co. started as one pot on a stove and a scent that wouldn't leave a friend's kitchen. Seven years on, we still pour every candle by hand, in a converted glasshouse on the edge of the city — just at a slightly larger stove.

2019

One scent, one kitchen

The first batch of Amber Dusk was poured in jam jars and given away at a friend's dinner party. Three people asked where to buy more. There was nowhere.

2021

The glasshouse

We took over a disused glasshouse behind a old print works, kept the skylights, and built our pouring bench under them. Natural light still tells us when a batch has set.

2023

The refill counter

Rather than sell more vessels, we opened a Saturday refill counter. Bring a jar back, we clean and refill it, at two-thirds of the price of a new one.

2026

Twelve pieces, on purpose

We keep the range deliberately small — five candles, two diffusers, a mist, an incense, two tools and one set. When something sells out, we let it stay sold out.

i.

Small runs

Twelve candles poured at a time, by one person, start to finish — never a production line.

ii.

Honest materials

Coconut-soy wax, cotton wicks, and fragrance built from essential oils we can name.

iii.

Kept, not thrown

Vessels come back to us for refilling, or go home with someone else's cuttings in them.

“We'd rather sell out of something good than keep something average in stock.”
— founding note, pinned above the pouring bench
