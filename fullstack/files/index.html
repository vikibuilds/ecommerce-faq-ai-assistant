01

Every wax is a coconut-soy blend, chosen for an even, tunnel-free burn that runs 55 to 60 hours a jar.

02

Fragrances are built in-house from essential oils and skin-safe aromatics, then rested for three weeks before pouring.

03

Vessels are kept — reused for offcuts, refilled at the atelier, or returned to the glassmaker at the end of their life.

The glasshouse, on the edge of town

We pour on Tuesdays. Everything else is timing.

Each fragrance rests for three weeks before it's poured, and every jar is hand-labelled the day it's packed. We keep runs small enough that a scent can sell out — and stay sold out — rather than reformulate it thinner to keep up.
